 197  define phrases en-US
 198    no-leaving:
 199      You look out at the rain. The rain looks back. Not yet.
 200    red-already-rung:
 201      The red rope is still swinging gently. Its bell has said its piece.
 202    white-too-early:
 203      You give the white rope a tug, then stop. The hymn board was clear
 204      about the order: red first.
 205    white-already-rung:
 206      The white rope is already swinging. One note per bell, for now.
 207    blue-too-early:
 208      Your hand closes on the blue rope and hesitates. Red, then white,
 209      then blue. The board was specific.
 210    hello-chord:
 211      You pull the blue rope. The third bell joins the other two, and for
 212      a long moment the tower holds a single chord over the wet fields.
 213      Below, the rain eases. Hello, Chord.
