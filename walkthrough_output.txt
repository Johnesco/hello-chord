Porch
The rain has chased you under the porch of a small stone church. Somewhere above, a bell tower waits for someone to give it a reason.

> south
You look out at the rain. The rain looks back. Not yet.

> north
Nave
Rows of pews face a plain altar. A hymn board hangs on a pillar. A small door in the corner leads east to the vestry, and a ladder climbs to a hatch in the ceiling.

> read board
The board reads, in worn brass numerals and a scrap of paper:
TODAY: NO HYMNS.
RING THE RED, THEN THE WHITE, THEN THE BLUE.
THE KEY IS ON THE HOOK.

> up
The hatch is locked.

> east
Vestry
A cramped room smelling of candle wax and damp wool. An iron hook is screwed into the wall at eye height. The nave is back to the west.

On the iron hook you see a brass key.

> take key
You take the brass key from the iron hook.

> west
Nave
Rows of pews face a plain altar. A hymn board hangs on a pillar. A small door in the corner leads east to the vestry, and a ladder climbs to a hatch in the ceiling.

> unlock hatch with key
You unlock the hatch with the brass key.

> open hatch
You open the hatch.

> up
Bell Loft
You climb through the hatch into the loft. Three ropes hang in a row, each rising into the dark toward a bell you cannot see.

You have risen to the rank of a Sexton.

> pull red rope
You haul on the red rope. Far above, a deep bell answers: one low, round note that goes on longer than seems reasonable.

> pull red rope
The red rope is still swinging gently. Its bell has said its piece.

> pull white rope
You pull the white rope. A second bell rings out, higher, and the two notes hang together in the air.

> score
You have scored 10 out of 20, earning you the rank of a Sexton.

> pull blue rope
You pull the blue rope. The third bell joins the other two, and for a long moment the tower holds a single chord over the wet fields. Below, the rain eases. Hello, Chord.

You have risen to the rank of a Bell-Ringer.
